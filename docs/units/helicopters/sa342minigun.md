## Background

The SA 342 Minigun represents a Gazelle variant configured for fire suppression and light attack missions, equipped with a side-mounted M134 minigun—the six-barrel 7.62mm rotary weapon capable of firing up to 6,000 rounds per minute. This configuration reflects the adaptation of the Gazelle platform for counterinsurgency and security operations where the primary targets are personnel, unarmored vehicles, and light structures rather than tanks.

This armament configuration has been employed by various Gazelle operators who modified their aircraft for specific operational requirements. The minigun's high rate of fire provides devastating suppressive capability for escort missions, landing zone preparation, or covering troop insertions and extractions. The relatively light weight of the 7.62mm ammunition allows the Gazelle to carry substantial ammunition loads despite its small size.

The trade-off for this firepower is the complete sacrifice of anti-armor capability. Without HOT missiles or rockets, the minigun-armed Gazelle cannot engage armored vehicles or fortified positions effectively. This makes the variant highly specialized for environments where air defense threats are minimal and the opposition consists primarily of irregular forces. In DCS, the SA 342 Minigun is flyable as part of the Gazelle module, offering a different tactical capability than the HOT-armed variants.
