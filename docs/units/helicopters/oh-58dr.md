## Background

The OH-58D(R) Kiowa Warrior represents the definitive production version of the armed reconnaissance helicopter, incorporating upgrades developed through decades of operational experience. The (R) designation indicates a retrofit standard applied to earlier OH-58D airframes, incorporating improved avionics, enhanced survivability features, and expanded weapons capability including compatibility with AGM-114 Hellfire missiles, 2.75-inch rockets (including laser-guided APKWS), and the .50 caliber M3P machine gun.

The Kiowa Warrior's operational concept centered on the scout-weapons team, pairing OH-58Ds with AH-64 Apaches in hunter-killer formations. The nimble Kiowa would scout ahead, detect targets using its Mast Mounted Sight while hiding behind terrain, then either engage with its own weapons or hand off targeting data to the heavier Apache. This teamwork allowed efficient use of the Apache's greater firepower while exploiting the Kiowa's superior agility and lower operating costs.

OH-58D(R) crews became masters of low-level reconnaissance and precision attack. In counterinsurgency operations in Iraq and Afghanistan, Kiowa teams provided persistent overwatch for ground convoys and patrols, engaging insurgent ambushes and IED-emplacement teams. The small helicopter could operate from confined forward arming and refueling points close to the action, responding rapidly to troops in contact. Though the type was retired from U.S. Army service in 2017, the Kiowa Warrior's tactics and techniques informed the development of future Army aviation concepts. In DCS, the OH-58D(R) is the high-fidelity player-flyable module featuring full systems modeling including the Mast Mounted Sight for target acquisition and laser designation.
