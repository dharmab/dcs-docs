## Background

The Ka-50 III "Black Shark 3" represents a modernized variant of the Ka-50 featuring significantly upgraded avionics and weapons compatibility. This version incorporates a digital moving map display, upgraded Shkval-M targeting system with improved optics and tracking capability, enhanced navigation systems including GLONASS satellite positioning, and compatibility with the improved 9M120-1 Ataka-V anti-tank missile offering greater range and penetration than the original Vikhr. The cockpit features modernized displays and reduced pilot workload through improved automation.

While the real-world Ka-50 production was limited and Russia transitioned to the two-seat Ka-52 Alligator as its primary attack helicopter, the Ka-50 III in DCS represents an evolution of the original Black Shark module incorporating avionics and weapons upgrades similar to those developed for the Ka-52 program. The Ataka missile system provides an alternative to the Vikhr, offering radio-command guidance and multiple warhead options including tandem HEAT for defeating explosive reactive armor, thermobaric for soft targets, and proximity-fused for engaging helicopters.

The Black Shark 3 maintains all the distinctive characteristics of the Ka-50 design: the coaxial rotor system providing exceptional hovering stability and eliminating tail rotor vulnerability, the single-seat cockpit with its emphasis on pilot automation, the powerful 2A42 autocannon, and the K-37-800 ejection system. In DCS, the Ka-50 III represents the latest evolution of the Black Shark module, featuring updated systems modeling and expanded weapons capability while retaining the authentic flight dynamics of the original.
